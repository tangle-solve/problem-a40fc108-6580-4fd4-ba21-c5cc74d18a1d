### ALGO-000027: Infinitude of the polynomial hierarchy
- **Subfield:** Structural complexity
- **Open:** Prove that \(\Sigma_k^P\ne\Sigma_{k+1}^P\) for every fixed \(k\), or establish a collapse.
- **Status:** Open since Stockmeyer’s 1976 formulation. Oracle worlds exhibit both collapses and infinite hierarchies, demonstrating limits of relativizing arguments.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines whether each additional alternation of existential and universal certification increases computational power.
- **Key works:** Stockmeyer (1976), *The Polynomial-Time Hierarchy*, DOI: 10.1016/0304-3975(76)90061-X; Baker, Gill, and Solovay (1975), *Relativizations of the P =? NP Question*.
- **Related:** Mathematical logic—quantifier-alternation hierarchies; knowledge representation—nested reasoning complexity.
